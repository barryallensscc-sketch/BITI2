Your questions, answered

Let's clear things up.

Who can enroll in a BITI course?

Our programs welcome students, graduates, career changers, and anyone eager to build practical digital skills. We help you find the right starting point.

Do I need prior experience in technology?

Not at all. Many of our foundation programs begin from zero and build confidence step by step.

Do you offer job placement support?

Yes. Our career team supports graduates with portfolio reviews, interview prep, and opportunities through our hiring network.

Can I attend classes online?

Several programs are available in live online formats, with access to mentors and learning resources.
